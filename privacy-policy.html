Privacy Policy

Rosie — Amplify Beta Extension  |  Effective date: October 7, 2026  |  Version 1.4.0

Rosie is an internal tool for Amplify Education team members only. It is not available to or intended for use by the general public.

1. What Rosie Does

Rosie is an internal Chrome extension that fetches, manages, and injects approved automation scripts ("bots") into specific work tools used by Amplify Education team members, including Amplify's classroom platform, Notion, Google Docs, Jira, Smartsheet, and Desmos. It provides a popup interface for enabling, disabling, and running those scripts, and sends anonymous usage telemetry to an internal Amplify Education tracking sheet.

3. Personally Identifiable Information

Rosie collects your name and email address from Google's OAuth userinfo endpoint when you first use the extension, using the openid, email, and profile OAuth scopes. A persistent anonymous identifier (UUID) is generated once at first use and preserved across reinstalls and devices.

What we store

  • Your name, email address, and UUID are stored in chrome.storage.sync so you appear as the same entry in the internal tracking sheet across all Chrome devices where you are signed into the same Google account.
  • Your telemetry state (last ping timestamp, error state, and the list of bots you have previously enabled and disabled) is stored in chrome.storage.local on your device only.

What we transmit

Your name, email address, UUID, current extension version, list of currently enabled bots, list of previously enabled and disabled bots, and the event reason are transmitted to an internal Amplify Education Google Apps Script endpoint at script.google.com, which upserts one row per user in a Google Sheet used for internal analytics. This data is transmitted when:

  • You open the Rosie popup (heartbeat event)
  • You toggle a bot on or off (toggle event, debounced to one ping per 10-second window)
  • You manually retry registration from the popup (retry event)

Purpose

This data is used exclusively for internal Amplify Education analytics to understand which automation tools are actively used by the team. It is not sold, licensed, or shared with any third party outside of Amplify Education.

4. Authentication Information

Rosie reads Notion session cookies — including token_v2 — on behalf of userscripts that make authenticated calls to Notion's internal API.

How cookie access is restricted

Cookie access is governed by a strict internal allowlist that enforces all four of the following checks before any cookie is read:

  1. The target URL must map to a permitted destination. Currently only notion.com and notion.so are permitted for cookie access. Jira (atlassian.net) and Smartsheet explicitly have cookie access denied.
  2. The request must originate from a permitted bridge host (amplify.com or docs.google.com). Notion's own pages are excluded as request origins to prevent Notion page scripts from accessing Notion session cookies.
  3. The cookie domain specified in the request must not exceed the permitted destination.
  4. The request must originate from a verified top-frame content script, not from a page script or iframe.

How cookies are used

Notion session cookies are read transiently, used to construct a single authenticated request to Notion's API on behalf of the running userscript, and discarded immediately. They are never stored by the extension, never written to chrome.storage, and never transmitted to any server other than Notion.

Google OAuth token

The extension uses a Google OAuth Bearer token to authenticate telemetry POST requests to the internal Amplify Apps Script endpoint. This token is obtained via Chrome's built-in chrome.identity API and is never stored by the extension. It is obtained fresh for each telemetry request and discarded after use.

5. User Activity

Rosie tracks which bots you have enabled and which you have previously enabled and then disabled. This information is stored locally in chrome.storage.local and transmitted to the internal Amplify Education tracking sheet as part of telemetry events described in Section 3.

No other user activity is tracked. Rosie does not record keystrokes, mouse movements, form inputs, page interactions, or any other behavioural data.

6. Website Content

Rosie injects approved automation scripts into web pages on the following domains:

  • *.amplify.com
  • docs.google.com
  • *.notion.com and *.notion.so
  • amplify-education.atlassian.net
  • *.desmos.com
  • *.smartsheet.com

Injected scripts automate tasks on these pages on behalf of the user. The Rosie extension infrastructure itself does not read, store, or transmit page content. Any data accessed or modified by an injected script is handled by that script in accordance with its individual documented purpose.

7. Data We Do Not Collect

  • Health or medical information
  • Financial or payment information
  • Personal communications (email, messages, chat)
  • Precise or approximate location
  • Full browsing history

Tab URLs are read transiently to determine which scripts match the current page. They are never stored, logged, or transmitted.

8. Data Sharing

Rosie does not sell, license, or transfer user data to any third party. The only external transmission of data is the telemetry payload described in Section 3, which is sent to an internal Amplify Education Google Apps Script endpoint. This data is used exclusively for internal Amplify Education analytics and is accessible only to authorised Amplify Education staff.

Rosie does not use or transfer user data for purposes unrelated to its single purpose as an internal automation script manager. Rosie does not use or transfer user data to determine creditworthiness or for lending purposes.

10. Your Rights

As an Amplify Education team member you may request deletion of your telemetry data from the internal tracking sheet by contacting your Amplify IT administrator. You can clear all locally stored extension data at any time by going to chrome://extensions → Details → Clear site data. Uninstalling the extension removes all locally stored data immediately.

11. Security

All external requests made by Rosie use HTTPS. Cookie access is restricted by a strict internal allowlist. Message routing between the extension's content scripts, the page context, and the background service worker validates the sender's identity and origin on every message. GM storage access is scoped per-script and per-host and cannot be accessed by scripts outside their permitted domains.

12. Changes to This Policy

If this policy is updated the effective date at the top of this page will be revised. Continued use of the extension after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

13. Contact

For questions about this privacy policy or to request data deletion, please contact your Amplify Education IT administrator or the Rosie extension maintainer at gepstein@amplify.com.
